# Model card: the models Chimera ships (as submitted, Oct 28, 2026)

Chimera is decision support for a hiring team: it screens a remote candidate for identity fraud and shows evidence; a person decides. Nothing here identifies a person by face or voice; the one face comparison is the ID check's 1:1 match of the applicant's own ID photo with the interview face, which needs the applicant's separate consent (A3 below). The models are the ones chosen before the Oct 25 feature freeze; what came after it (evidence pictures, a PDF report, a watchlist admin page) changes no model, scoring rule or cut-off. Edits made on the Watchlist page change the list the A3 check matches against, never its rule: which rows match, how strongly (a row's attribution, or a note that the profile was impersonated, sets a match's strength) and the ring numbers; a retired row keeps its place but matches nothing. Until a change is made on that page, or after "Use the original file" sends the Console back to the configured file, every score is the same as at the freeze. Licence of this copy: see `LICENSE.md` (`docs/SOURCES.md` lists the models, data and main libraries with their licences). The models in use were chosen on Oct 24 from the recording session (`docs/DECISIONS.md`; `docs/EVALUATION.md` section 21).

## A1: face-swap check

| | |
|---|---|
| Models | Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct (Apache-2.0) with two LoRA adapters trained by Darpan, Run 3 (Celeb-DF v2) and Run 5z (FaceForensics++ c23 with half the training scenes re-encoded like a meeting; seed 3407; it replaced Run 5 on Oct 24), and GenD (CLIP ViT-L/14; Yermakov et al., WACV 2026, the authors' weights). Scores combined by the average of their percentiles among 315 reference real videos, each model against its own reference scores (`data/detector_reference_v5z.json`). Run 5 and Run 5z-s2 stay available behind `CHIMERA_RUN5_ADAPTER` with `CHIMERA_DETECTOR_REFERENCE`. |
| Input | 256 × 256 face crops (MediaPipe, 1.3× box) from an interview recording at 2 frames per second, or every 0.5 s in the live check (Run 5z + GenD only, averaged over the last 8 faces). |
| Output | A 0–1 combined score per recording; moderate and strong cut-offs; the live check's running average against its cut-off. For the reviewer, both checks also keep their five most suspicious moments, at least 2 s apart, as pictures (the face crop the models scored and the whole frame; `chimera/evidence_frames.py`), shown in the Console and the PDF report; they do not change the score or the evidence text the agents read. |
| Training data | Celeb-DF v2 (research terms, no redistribution) for Run 3; FaceForensics++ c23 (research terms) for Run 5z, half of it passed through an H.264 encode at Zoom-like bits per pixel. The adapters are therefore research-only artefacts; they cannot be redistributed under a permissive licence. |
| Evaluation | **On Zoom** (`docs/EVALUATION.md` section 21; one person, 640×360, Oct 24): all 19 live face swaps (Deep-Live-Cam, FaceFusion's webcam mode with its occlusion mask on and off) and all 6 combined attacks flagged at the strong cut-off; 8 of 8 pre-recorded swaps at moderate (5 strong; AUC 0.93 against held-out genuine takes); 8 of 8 lip-sync clips at strong; 2 of 10 held-out genuine daylight takes flagged at moderate (1 strong). **Public footage** (sections 5–7, 19, 20): DFDC sample AUC 0.860 with Run 5z in the trio (0.849 with Run 5); Celeb-DF++ 21 new methods mean AUC 0.780 (0.766 after a shared re-encode); FaceForensics++ test as shipped 0.983 (Run 5z alone). |

Run 5z is weaker than Run 5 on clean reenactment (Celeb-DF++ reenactment 0.544 against 0.627 alone). Replays of real footage show a real face and are not flagged. One person's footage, one camera, one meeting app. |

The evening calls set nothing. |

## A1: voice-clone check

| | |
|---|---|
| Models | Spectra-0 (lab260, Apache-2.0; the default since Oct 24) or DF Arena 500M (Speech-Arena-2025; **non-commercial licence**; opt-in with `CHIMERA_VOICE_MODEL=dfarena500m`). Silero VAD (MIT) finds speech. |
| Input | The candidate's own audio track when the meeting app records one; 4.04 s windows 2 s apart that are at least 75% speech; recording score = mean of the top quarter. |
| Evaluation | **On Zoom** (section 21): Spectra-0 flagged all 28 cloned voices at its strong cut-off (Qwen3-TTS 5, XTTS-v2 5, the lip-sync clips' 8, live Seed-VC 4, the combined attack's 6) and 1 of 10 held-out genuine takes; DF Arena 500M flagged 13 of the 28 at strong, none of the Qwen3-TTS clones. In-the-Wild (300 real, 300 fake): AUC 0.997 (DF Arena 500M) and 0.995 (Spectra-0) (section 8). Cut-offs from the 38 daylight calls: Spectra-0 3.6261 / 4.0381 log-odds, bounds 7.7% / 2.6%. |
| Known problems | Spectra-0 also flags played-back audio: all 8 pre-recorded swaps (Darpan's own voice recorded Oct 18 and played through a virtual cable) and 1 of 3 replays, so its flag means a synthetic or played-back voice, not cloning alone. No published cut-off transfers to a webcam mic through a meeting app; each model needs its own reference from the deployment channel. The microphone moves genuine scores more than anything else. One speaker so far. |

## A3: application and OSINT checks

Code only: exact matching after normalization against a watchlist with ring clustering (`docs/EVALUATION.md` section 2: 18.4% day-one catch on the real watchlist, aggregates only), hosting and VPN networks, VoIP numbers, account ages, stated country against the IP's, saved lookups (GitHub, IPinfo Lite, Twilio, RDAP) that run only with the applicant's consent and never on synthetic identifiers, and web leads for the reviewer (info only). The Console labels this family "Application & OSINT"; the analyst's prompt and name in LangFlow are unchanged.

**ID check (Oct 24, minimal version, `chimera/idcheck.py`).** When the case holds an ID document (a JPEG or PNG of a passport page or ID card): RapidOCR reads the machine-readable lines; their check digits (ICAO Doc 9303) are verified, with a failure scored moderate (a misread can fail one too); the name in them is compared with the application's, allowing small slips (mismatch moderate). Nothing else is read into the evidence: no nationality, date of birth or sex, and the document number is checked but never written. With the applicant's separate consent, the ID photo is matched 1:1 against the interview face: MediaPipe finds the faces, InsightFace's ArcFace (w600k_r50, non-commercial) embeds them, and the 80th percentile of the cosine similarity over up to 40 frames decides (0.35 or more is a match, under 0.20 a different person, scored strong). Checked on known pairs (Oct 24, `scripts/idcheck_calibrate.py`): Darpan's own Oct 18 webcam frame on a specimen page against his 10 held-out Zoom takes of Oct 24 scored 0.647 to 0.724 (all matches); ten FLUX portraits of nobody on specimen pages against the same takes scored -0.016 to 0.186 (all 100 pairs "different person"). One genuine person and ten impostors is a small check, not an error rate. Chimera never compares a face with anyone but the applicant's own ID. Tested on fictional Utopia specimen pages (`scripts/make_specimen_id.py`); not tested on real documents, real scans or photos of IDs, or on document forgery beyond wrong check digits (a photo substituted into a real document passes the match and the check digits; detecting that needs document forensics, future work). Face matching is biometric data: in a real deployment it needs the applicant's written consent and legal review (BIPA in Illinois, GDPR article 9).

## The agents

qwen3-coder 30B (Ollama, 16K context) in LangFlow: three analysts, an Orchestrator, a Reporter. They compute nothing; code writes the score lines and the Contradictions section, and checks every citation. The analysts' one Chimera tool is the Case Reader. LangFlow's Agent component also switches on two built-in tools by default, Current Date and Calculator, and they stayed on in every run measured: in the last saved run of each of the ten synthetic cases (Oct 25) an agent asked for the date in 7 and none used the calculator (`docs/EVALUATION.md` section 24). Neither reaches the network. Turning them off is a step for after the demo. Pass rate (both checks) since code took over the Contradictions section: 30 of 30 runs on the cases the prompts were tuned on and 30 of 30 on a held-out batch, up from 27 and 24 (`docs/EVALUATION.md` sections 13, 14 and 22; synthetic cases), and 9 of 9 on the three real-call demo cases once the Case Reader took the case from the run's session (section 23).

## Intended use and limits

- Decision support for a hiring team, with a person making the decision; never an automatic rejection.
- Not for identifying people; not for inferring nationality, ethnicity, accent or origin (none is an input).
- Research prototype: the cut-offs hold for one person's calls on one camera and meeting app in daylight; false-alarm rates on other faces, voices, cameras, rooms and accents, and behaviour on real applicants, are not established (`docs/EVALUATION.md` sections 17 and 21).
- Legal review before any deployment (README, legal note).
- What is still to do, from document forensics to calibration for each room: README, Future work.
