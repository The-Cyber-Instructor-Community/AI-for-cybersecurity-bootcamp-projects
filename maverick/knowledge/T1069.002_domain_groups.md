# T1069.002 Permission Groups Discovery: Domain Groups

## What it looks like
Commands such as `net group "Domain Admins" /domain` list members of privileged domain groups.

## Signals that point to malicious
- Run by a non-administrator, or by an administrator account behaving unusually (new device, odd hours, no change record).
- Parent process is cmd.exe or PowerShell launched by an Office or email process.
- Follows a suspicious sign-in or an encoded PowerShell execution.

## Signals that point to benign
- Run by an IT administrator with an approved change ticket, from their usual device.

## Triage guidance
Dual-use: the same command is routine for admins and a classic attacker discovery step. A privileged role alone is not evidence that the activity is legitimate; look for a change record.
